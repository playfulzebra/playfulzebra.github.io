PFM

Personal Finance Manager

PFM is a private household finance app used by two people to see their own bank accounts, spending, debts and investments in one place. It is not available to the public.

Privacy policy

Last updated: November 23, 2026

What data PFM uses

PFM connects to the users' own financial accounts through Plaid, only after the account holder signs in to their bank and gives consent in Plaid's connection screen. PFM receives account names and balances, transactions, credit card and loan details, and investment holdings.

Where it is stored

The data is stored only on the household's own computer. It is not kept on any other server, and the bank sign-in details are never seen or stored by PFM.

Sharing

The data is used only to show the account holders their own finances. It is never shared with, sold to, or used for advertising by anyone.

Deleting your data

Disconnecting a bank in the app ends PFM's access to that bank through Plaid and removes its saved data. Access can also be revoked at any time through Plaid (my.plaid.com) or the bank itself.
